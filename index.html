BIG BOT Backups

Encrypted recovery backups, stored in the owner's Google Drive.

BIG BOT Backups is a locally run backup tool operated by its owner for personal use. It is not a public service and does not offer accounts, subscriptions, or a sign-up form.

What the tool does

After the owner authorizes Google Drive access, the tool uses rclone crypt to encrypt recovery archives on the owner's computer before uploading them. It downloads a copy to check the contents and support recovery.

Each backup is written as a new snapshot. The backup routine does not overwrite or automatically delete earlier snapshots. The owner manages retention and deletion.

Why Google Drive access is requested

The tool requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. Its backup workflow creates and accesses its own backup files and folders, reads their metadata, and checks available storage. It does not request unrestricted access to the owner's entire Drive.

The owner stays in control

Google authorization is completed in the owner's browser. Access can be reviewed or removed through Google Account connections. Removing access stops future authorized Drive operations; it does not delete existing backups.

See the privacy policy for data use, local credential protection, and deletion details.

Contact and availability

The operator and intended user are the same person. This informational site does not collect support requests or provide a public customer service. The owner manages the tool through their local installation and Google Account settings.

This description covers the backup design. It does not confirm that an account is connected, a backup has succeeded, or recovery has been verified.

BIG BOT Backups is an independent personal project and is not a Google product.
